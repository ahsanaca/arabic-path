Privacy Policy — Arabic Academy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Arabic Academy ("the app") is a language-learning app. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The short version: your learning progress stays on your device unless you choose to create an account, we don't run ads or trackers, and we never sell your data.

Data collected on-device (always)

By default, the app stores your course progress — XP, streaks, which chapters and exercises you've completed — locally on your device only, using browser local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you create an account (see below). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Optional account & cloud sync

You can optionally create an account (email + password) to sync your progress across devices. If you do:

  • Your email address and password are handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). We never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Your progress data (XP, streaks, completed chapters/exercises) is stored in a private cloud database (Firestore), tied to your account. Security rules restrict this data so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it — no other user, and no one at Arabic Academy, has standing access to it.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time by contacting us (see below).

If you skip account creation, none of this applies — the app works entirely offline with local-only progress.

Microphone & speech recognition

The Speaking practice feature uses your microphone to let you compare your pronunciation against the target phrase. When you use it:

  • Audio is sent to your device's own built-in speech-recognition service (provided by your device's operating system — typically Google on Android) to convert your speech into text.
  • The app itself does not record, store, or transmit your voice audio to our own servers. We only receive the resulting text transcript, which is used momentarily to score your pronunciation attempt and is not saved.
  • Your device's speech-recognition provider handles that audio under its own privacy policy — see Google's Privacy Policy if your device uses Google's recognizer.

Microphone access is only requested when you tap the record button in the Speaking tab, and you can deny or revoke it at any time in your device settings without losing access to the rest of the app.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics SDKs that build a profile of you.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
Note for future updates: if the app later adds paid/premium features, any payment processing will be handled entirely by the app store (Google Play Billing) or a payment processor such as RevenueCat/Stripe — we do not directly collect or store payment card details, and this policy will be updated to name the processor used.

Children's privacy

Arabic Academy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account without appropriate consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

Data retention

Local progress data persists until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Cloud account data persists until you request deletion. We don't keep backups beyond what's needed for the service to function.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
